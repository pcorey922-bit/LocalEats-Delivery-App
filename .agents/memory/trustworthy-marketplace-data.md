---
name: Trustworthy marketplace data
description: LocalEats must distinguish real restaurant/payment activity from examples and unfinished integrations.
---

LocalEats must not present invented restaurants as real partners or claim that a payment was processed when it was not. Any payment testing must be labeled clearly as test mode, and unfinished third-party integrations must be identified as not connected.

**Why:** the user explicitly required an honest marketplace and payment experience; false partner or payment claims would mislead customers and local businesses.

**How to apply:** do not seed fictional restaurants into customer-facing listings. For future payment work, keep test mode visually unmistakable and clearly mark where real payment credentials/integration are still needed.
